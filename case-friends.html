Case 03 · Making friends · Shipped

4 in 10people who go to a meetupnow leave with a friend.

Role
Sole product designer
Team
1 front-end + 1 back-end engineer, PM
Design time
2 weeks
01The question

Would people make friends on Misfits at all?

This didn’t start as a problem to fix. It was my manager’s idea: Misfits should feel more social, and the first step was a simple question: if we let people make friends here, would they? So we ran it as an experiment, with the smallest feature that could answer it.

From the brief
  • My manager’s idea: let people make friends on Misfits
  • Run it as an experiment, with the smallest feature possible
My decisions
  • Only people you’ve met can be added
  • The people-you-met screen after a meetup
  • The Friends filter on the home screen
  • The profile: two big numbers instead of a bio
02The signal

People were already curious about each other.

50%

of people on a meetup page tapped to see who else was coming.

20%+

opened other people’s profiles, before and after meetups.

0

ways to stay in touch after. A profile, and nothing you could do with it.

03The insight

Asking someone to be your friend is awkward. Tapping a button isn’t.

After a good evening, walking up to someone and saying “let’s be friends” takes courage most people don’t have. A request after the meetup is an easy way to say “I liked hanging out with you.”

04The decision

Only people you’ve actually met.

You can only add someone you’ve been to a meetup with. That keeps it safe and private, and makes it something Instagram can’t do. Every friendship on Misfits means two people met in real life.

×

No requests from strangers.

×

No “people you may know”.

→

So almost nobody says no.

05The design

Two small pieces, nothing more.

After a meetup, the people you met are waiting on one screen, one tap to add each. Then a Friends filter on the home screen shows the meetups your friends are going to, so the friendship has somewhere to go.

  1. Only people from that meetup
  2. One tap to add
  3. Friends filter on the home screen
06What I tried

A profile like every other profile.

The first profile had a bio and small stats. It looked like any other app. We swapped them for two big numbers: meetups attended and friends made.

07The impact
4 in 10

people who went to a meetup made a friend.

With no big feature and no push, people added each other on their own. Almost every request was accepted.

08And it mattered

People show up more when a friend is going.

We looked at the same people’s bookings. When a friend had booked the same meetup, they turned up 8 times out of 10. When no friend was going, it was about 4 out of 10.

09What’s next

An experiment, not a finished feature.

We built the smallest thing that could answer one question, and the answer was yes: people do make friends here. Now there’s a base to build more social features on.
